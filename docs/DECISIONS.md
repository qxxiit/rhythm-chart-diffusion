| 2026-09-26 | Pattern clarity (§4.11-2b) is defined by periodicity, not by named rules: a run is a lane motif of p <= 8 events repeating at one snap (gap <= 1 beat), at least max(2p, p + 4) events, reported at its shortest period. Parameters to freeze after the human baselines, before model output |
